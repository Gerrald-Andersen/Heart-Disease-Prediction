# ❤️ Heart Disease Prediction WebApp
[Streamlit WebApp](https://heart-disease-prediction-gerrald-andersen.streamlit.app/)

## 🧭 Purpose

This application was developed to help users predict the likelihood of having heart disease.  
By providing a machine learning–based risk prediction, the app allows individuals to take preventive actions early.  
If a high risk is detected, users can consult a doctor for further diagnosis and treatment before the condition worsens.

---

## ⚙️ What I Did

The development process involved several key stages:

1. **Task Plan**  
   - Before initiating the project, I carefully outlined a task plan to ensure clarity, focus, and efficiency throughout the development process. The goal was to build a predictive dashboard that could assist users in identifying potential heart disease risks.

2. **Data Collection**  
   - The dataset was obtained from [Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/johnsmith88/heart-disease-dataset), which contains patient health records used for predicting heart disease presence.

3. **Data Cleansing**  
   - Cleaned and processed the dataset to ensure data quality, consistency, and suitability for model training.  
   - Handled missing values, encoded categorical features, and normalized numeric values.

4. **Model Development**  
   - Several models were tested, including **Logistic Regression**, **Random Forest Classifier**, **MLP (Multi-Layer Perceptron)**, and **Decision Tree**.  
   - After comparison, the **Random Forest Classifier** achieved the best performance and was selected for deployment.

5. **Web Application Deployment**  
   - Developed an interactive web interface using [Streamlit]([https://streamlit.io/](https://heart-disease-prediction-gerrald-andersen.streamlit.app/)) to allow users to input their medical data and instantly receive predictions on their heart disease risk level.

---

## 📊 Results

The final **Random Forest Classifier** model achieved an **accuracy of 91%**, making the predictions highly reliable and trustworthy.  
This demonstrates that the model can effectively identify individuals at risk of heart disease, empowering users to make informed health decisions.

---

## 🚀 Future Improvements

Planned enhancements include:

- **Expanded Disease Prediction**  
  - Extend the system beyond heart disease to include conditions such as **diabetes**, **stroke**, and **kidney disease**.

- **Model Optimization**  
  - Perform hyperparameter tuning and cross-validation to further enhance accuracy and generalization.

- **User Profile Management**  
  - Add functionality for saving user history and tracking prediction results over time.

- **Integration with Health APIs**  
  - Connect to APIs that provide live health data from wearable devices (e.g., Fitbit, Apple Health) for real-time monitoring.

- **Cloud Deployment**  
  - Host the application on a scalable cloud platform for better accessibility and performance.
